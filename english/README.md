# How ?
The idea of this system is the same as `BitTorrent`, imagine a discipline, suppose that I want to study trigonometry but I know nothing about it. A teacher with a diploma, member of the system, is busy and can't teach me this school branch, but a student from the association or system is fine with teaching me the field. First, the initial teacher will test his aptitudes to teach. Second, if he passes, he then can teach this subbranch of mathematics to other students. Thus, mathematics are subdivided in several smaller branches that can be more easily taught from student to student.

For the system to work as intended, a subsystem working on credits is implemented for the members who teach the fields they've mastered and study the ones in which they need help. The disponibilities and the access to study are subdivided, that way, it's easier to learn and relearn some subbranches depending on our own initial knowledges.

This system is evolving and grow depending their members and its capacity.

`I see this system as a patch, a subsystem in the "society" system that works in parallel to the classic study system`
